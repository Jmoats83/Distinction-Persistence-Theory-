# Theory Summary (Transmission-safe)
**Joshua D. Moats** — Originator

Distinction–Persistence Theory (DPT) treats **persistence of distinguishability** as the primary quantity of interest.

DPT does **not** replace conservation laws; it overlays them by asking which structures remain identifiable under the dynamics those laws permit.

---

## Core idea

- A **distinction** is anything that can be told apart (structure).
- **Persistence** is the fact that it stays tell-apart-able under legitimate pressure.
- The most real structures are those whose distinguishability survives aggressive, lawful attempts to erase it.

---

## What DPT aims to do

1. Provide a minimal axiomatic definition of “exists” in terms of persistence.
2. Define overhead and stability criteria that predict reconfiguration (phase transitions, institutional shifts, identity shifts).
3. Specify scaling limits that force innovation or collapse.
4. Create bridges to formal domains (QFT, information theory, systems) that are falsifiable and not flexible.
