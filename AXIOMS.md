# DPT Core Axioms
**Joshua D. Moats** — Originator

This file records the axiomatic spine of Distinction–Persistence Theory (DPT) in a canonical, repository-stable form.

---

## Axiom I — Identity Existence Condition

An entity **exists** (is a persistent distinction) if and only if there exists a projection operator **Π** such that the persistence functional **P(Π) > 0**.

Intuition: existence is not a label; it is *survival of distinguishability* under legitimate pressure.

---

## Axiom II — Overhead Optimization Principle

Systems spontaneously reconfigure when an alternative configuration provides a lower canonical overhead (i.e., a lower cost of maintaining distinguishability).

Intuition: stable structure is “least distinction effort” subject to constraints.

---

## Axiom III — Congestion–Innovation Limit

As scaling pressure rises, a system must either innovate structurally or collapse (loss of persistence).

Intuition: growth forces new compression, new architecture, or failure.
